一、启动回归：根因与修复
----------------------------------------------------------------------
现象：签名安装后启动，进程在写日志 [PKG] 后即退出，UI 未创建。
证据（Windows 事件日志 Application，02:06:18 / 02:06:19）：
  .NET Runtime 1026: The process was terminated due to an unhandled exception.
  Exception Info: System.Runtime.InteropServices.MarshalDirectiveException:
    Cannot marshal 'parameter #2': Cannot marshal managed types when the runtime marshalling system is disabled.
    at WinUI2Demo.Program.GetWindowThreadProcessId(IntPtr hwnd, UInt32& pid)
    at WinUI2Demo.Program.ForegroundDesc(IntPtr fg) Program.cs:line 1309
    at WinUI2Demo.Program.LogForeground(String tag) Program.cs:line 1315
    at WinUI2Demo.Program.Main(String[] args) Program.cs:line 314
根因：项目启用 DisableRuntimeMarshalling=true，上一轮加焦点日志时用了 out uint 的 DllImport，运行期封送被禁用 → 启动基线日志直接抛异常。
修复：改为 blittable 指针签名（unsafe uint* pid），调用处用 unsafe 取地址；不启用运行期封送。
文件：src/WinUI2Demo/Program.cs:193、1304-1311
